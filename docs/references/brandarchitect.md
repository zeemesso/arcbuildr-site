## 3. Conversion mechanics

- **CTA wording:** four different phrases for similar actions: "Reserve My Seat" (nav + hero), "Get My Ticket" (General), "Contact Us on WhatsApp" (VIP and VVIP). Total 5 to 6 buttons. Lower tiers buy in a checkout; top tiers talk to a person.
- **Qualification:** VVIP is application and review only, capped at 10 (stated in the FAQ, not on the card). That is the filter. General tier is open.
- **Form fields:** none on page. Applications go through a prefilled WhatsApp message (a one-tap, no-form route).
- **Flow:** low tier = ticket checkout. High tier = WhatsApp conversation, reviewed individually.
- **Scarcity/urgency:** "Limited availability", "Limited seats" on VVIP, "speakers revealed closer to the event". No countdown, no price clock.
- **Proof:** speaker credentials as numbers (monthly revenue, audience size, views per reel, subscriber count), one aggregate stat. No attendee testimonials, no past-event proof (first edition **UNVERIFIED**).
- **Risk reversal:** none. "All sales final", transfers allowed with approval.
- **Price display:** hidden on all three tiers. Tiers differ by access, not by listed price.
- **Page inconsistencies:** the stat says 8 speakers; 9 cards show. Shows how an unchecked number hurts trust.

## 4. Copy patterns

- Section titles are promise phrases: "Every lever to scale your business", "Build a brand that generates real cash".
- Speaker line = name + one concrete number. No adjectives.
- The offer parts are named by function: Offer Architecture, Brand Positioning, Content That Converts, Funnels, Sales Systems, Roadmap. A "business chain" frame, not a content-only frame.
- Tiers sell by exclusivity: access, proximity to the host, a personalised deliverable ("90-day roadmap", "3 SOPs"). Quote (under 15 words): "Choose Your Experience".
- Outcome is a revenue number; process is a day-by-day agenda.

## 6. Relevant to Arcbuildr

- Tiered access with the top tier reached by a message (not a form) mirrors how a high-ticket service should convert Instagram traffic: a DM or WhatsApp button with prefilled text.
- Hide prices, sell by what each level includes and by who works on it. Matches the "no prices" rule.
- State the cap and the review step ("each application is reviewed") to make the top option feel selective.
- Authority by one number per person: for Arcbuildr, one concrete outcome per team member or past client once real.
- A day-by-day or week-by-week agenda block works as a "how the engagement runs" block.
- Business-chain framing (offer, positioning, content, funnel, sales) supports the "business-driven client" claim.

**Avoid:** several different CTA phrases for the same action; unverified numbers that contradict each other; a page with zero attendee or client proof; "all sales final" on a service.

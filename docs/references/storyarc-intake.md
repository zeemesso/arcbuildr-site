# Story ARC intake form (Michelle Lo Horton)

URL: https://forms.thestoryarc.io/work-with-me (tracking parameters stripped). Linked from her Instagram bio.
Captured 2026-11-02 from the form's own page data (Zite, Next.js). Nothing was submitted.

## Mechanics

- One question per screen, "Next" button, back button. Intro line sets the time cost: "this takes ~3 minutes".
- 14 steps, all required except "main online presence":
  1. Full name
  2. Email + phone (country picker)
  3. Country
  4. Instagram handle; main online presence (Instagram / LinkedIn / Website)
  5. Describe yourself in 3 to 5 sentences ("the kinds of stories you might have to tell")
  6. Annual revenue (pre-revenue to $10M+)
  7. Team size (solo to 20+)
  8. Have you tried creating content? (No: don't know what to say / scared it's cringe / no time / can't package my story. Yes: posting doesn't work / keep starting and stopping)
  9. How content will help you (tell my story, audience, thought leader, partners, awareness, brand)
  10. Growth target this year (none / 20 to 40% / 2x / 10x)
  11. On camera? (yes / can try / voice only / absolutely not); existing b-roll?
  12. What must happen in 6 to 12 months to call it a win?
  13. Budget over 3 to 6 months ($900 to 2k / four figures / five figures / not yet)
  14. Timeline to start (ASAP / 30 to 45 days / 2 to 3 months / just exploring)
- Routing at the end:
  - Lowest budget answer goes to a cheaper product (a 60-minute "Power Hour") with its own Calendly.
  - "Not yet" on budget, or any start other than ASAP, goes to a "Not ready" ending (no booking).
  - Low revenue goes to the Power Hour ending.
  - Everyone else gets "strong fit" copy and the main Calendly, then a prep page: bring your numbers, have decision-makers on the call, camera on, reschedule if you can't decide.

## What Arcbuildr takes

- Two optional qualifying questions on our form: "Are you ready to be on camera?" and "When do you want to start?". They map to our "not for you" list and land in the Notion Goal text, so the database needs no new columns.
- Not taken now: revenue, budget and growth questions (no prices on the site; the form stays short for Instagram traffic), the multi-screen flow (more JavaScript on phones) and the budget routing (we have one offer).
- Later, when the digital product exists: route low-fit answers to it, the way her form routes to the Power Hour.

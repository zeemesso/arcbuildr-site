## Recommended section order: high-ticket service landing page

Mobile first. Instagram traffic to a single page, one outcome: apply or book.

1. Sticky header with the brand and one CTA (also a bottom bar on phones). Same wording everywhere.
2. Hero: who it is for + the outcome + how long; one short line for the biggest objection; CTA.
3. Authority line: founders' real, approved facts (hold anything unapproved).
4. Problem in the buyer's voice: 3 to 4 short pains, then the reframe ("your content is fine, your brand isn't").
5. Anti-agency stance: you work with the founders directly (one line or short block).
6. What you get: the five deliverables as numbered parts (01 to 05), each with a one-line job. No prices.
7. How it runs: phased timeline (week by week) with a concrete deliverable per phase.
8. Proof block: case or result tiles when real data exists. Until then a "first clients" scarcity line is honest; no invented numbers. CTA after it.
9. Who it is for / not for: 4 bullets each.
10. How to start: 3 or 4 steps after you apply (reply, short call, audit, kickoff).
11. Application form (short): name, email, Instagram handle, "who do you serve and what problem do you solve", "what are you struggling with", posting rhythm dropdown. Or a prefilled DM/WhatsApp button as the lighter route.
12. FAQ: fit, time commitment, what we need from you, platforms, what happens if it is not a fit, cost handling ("pricing is shared after the call").
13. Closing CTA + "still unsure? message us" human fallback + footer disclaimer.

CTA placement: header, hero, after proof, after how-it-runs, form, final. Maximum 5 to 6 on one page.

## Future digital-product page (AI personal-brand toolkit, Darija support)

Separate from the service page; reached from a hub or link-in-bio button.

1. Fixed header with the product name and "Get Access".
2. Hero: one claim, a 2-sentence mechanism, price block, CTA, "instant access" micro-line. Price only when Zyade approves.
3. 30 to 45 second demo video, no sound needed (captions).
4. 3-step mechanism (input, AI step, saved result).
5. Pain and "what if" turn.
6. What's included: items named by what they are, each with one line on its job. Add a Darija script and caption line.
7. Setup in minutes: numbered video steps and a time claim we can prove.
8. FAQ: paid AI accounts needed, technical skill, no audience yet, mobile, subscription or one-time, updates, refund policy, Darija support, language mix.
9. Repeat price and CTA; refund/guarantee line stated clearly and once (avoid conflicting wording).
10. Footer disclaimer: results depend on use.

Ladder to keep in mind (Sterling): free or low-price product, then waitlist for the premium service.

## Avoid (all sites)

- Invented value totals, generated scarcity and toasts, unattributed "multi-millionaire" claims.
- Several phrasings for the same CTA; contradictory guarantee text.
- Proof borrowed from other people (speaker lineups) or follower marquees we cannot fill.
- Pages with no proof at all, unless the founders' own public presence carries them.
